About This Collection

The body as architecture. Fabric as skin.

Void & Shroud interrogates the boundary between garment and body — where one ends and the other begins. Each piece in this collection wraps the wearer in layers of raw, unfinished cotton and linen, evoking ancient ritual dress, industrial packaging, and post-apocalyptic anonymity.

The artist approach here is total: no conventional cuts, no pattern symmetry. These are pieces sculpted around the body in motion, photographed live on the runway and translated into wearable originals.

Sculptural Avant-Garde Monochrome Raw Textile

"To cover the face is not to hide — it is to let the garment speak for what the body cannot."
