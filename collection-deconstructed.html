About This Collection

Rules of tailoring — broken by design.

Deconstructed questions everything a suit is supposed to be: structured, symmetrical, finished. Here, seams are exposed as decoration, shoulders are deconstructed into draped panels, and trouser legs exceed every convention of cut.

Three artists — working independently in Korea, Japan, and Spain — each interpreted the brief "destroy the suit" without consultation. The results share a spirit but no aesthetic DNA.
