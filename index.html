Behind the Brand

Art. Identity. Scarcity. Culture.

What We Are

Fashion as a cultural statement, not just a product.

BLiminal was born from a simple but radical idea: what if clothing could carry the same weight as a gallery painting? What if wearing a piece of fabric meant owning something irreplaceable — a collaboration between a collector and an artist?

We curate partnerships with independent artists across every discipline — from textile sculpture to digital print — and transform their vision into limited-edition wearable art. Every drop is a cultural event. Every garment holds a story.

Every item is one of a kind.

What Sets Us Apart

Five Differentiators

No other brand combines all five of these — and that's intentional.

01
✦
Artist-Led Collections

Every piece originates from an artist's vision, not a trend report. We provide the brief — they bring the soul.

02
◎
1/1 & Serialised Runs

True scarcity — each buyer owns something unique or numbered. No reprints. No restocks. Ever.

03
◈
Wearable Art Concept

We sit in the gallery space, not just the clothing rack. Fashion finally earns its place alongside fine art.

04
⊞
Archive & Rarity System

Full tracking of each piece's provenance and artist history — a permanent record for every garment we release.

05
◉

Every garment links to the artist's story, process, and interview. The clothing is just the beginning.
