About This Collection

When the garment becomes the art.

Ethereal Form is BLiminal's most ambitious collection — four one-of-one couture works that exist between gallery installation and wearable clothing. Each piece took between 200 and 600 hours of construction.

From the swirling geological strata of the earth-tone gown to the deep-space organza sculpture of the dark ruffled piece — every work in this collection carries an exhibition history alongside its wearability.
